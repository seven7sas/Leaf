  - In `Timer.next()`, cleanly stops `prev.stop()`, resets `tick = 0`, starts `nextEvent.start()`, and selects a new random next event.

5. Part 5: PasteBuilder Fast Remove, In-Memory Clipboard Cache & Multi-Source BFS Smoothing (IceSchematics & CoreLib)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Files:
  - `D:\iceworld\IceWorld\IceSchematics\src\main\java\dev\sevensas\iceschematics\schem\SchematicManager.java`
  - `D:\iceworld\IceWorld\IceSchematics\src\main\java\dev\sevensas\iceschematics\schem\PasteBuilder.java`
  - `D:\iceworld\IceWorld\IceSchematics\src\main\java\dev\sevensas\iceschematics\util\Smoothing.java`
  - `D:\iceworld\IceWorld\CoreLib\src\main\java\dev\sevensas\corelib\online\OnlineTracker.java`
- Schematic Clipboard Cache:
  - In `SchematicManager`, added `clipboardCache` (`Map<String, CachedClipboard>`) caching parsed WorldEdit `Clipboard` objects keyed by schematic name and file `lastModified`.
  - Added `cloneClipboard(Clipboard)` with `copy.setOrigin(source.getOrigin())` using `BlockArrayClipboard` and `ForwardExtentCopy` to duplicate clipboards in memory without disk I/O.
  - In `PasteBuilder.paste()`, retrieves cached clipboards via `SchematicManager.get().getClipboard(name)`.
- PasteBuilder removeBlocks / removeLiquid Optimization:
  - Cached `CAN_BE_WATERLOGGED` (`ConcurrentHashMap<BlockType, Boolean>`) to avoid calling `getProperty("waterlogged")` string lookups on types that cannot be waterlogged.
  - Early-returns on air blocks (`clipState.getBlockType().getMaterial().isAir()` and `worldState.getBlockType().getMaterial().isAir()`), immediately bypassing ~180,000 empty checks.
  - Y bounds for liquid removal span `origin.y() - removeRadius .. origin.y() + removeRadius` bounded by world min/max height, ensuring that water hovering above the -5 Y pit is cleanly removed.
  - Replaced intermediate `BlockVector3` object allocations with integer coordinate offsets.
- Smoothing Algorithm Optimization (Smoothing.java):
  - Compact "Ellipse Up" Pit Architecture & Overhang Protection:
    - Floor Alignment: `pitFloorY = baseFloorY` aligns the pit floor flush with red wool (`RED_WOOL`) and foundation blocks instead of dropping 1 block lower.
    - Technical Block Filter: `isEmptyOrTechnical` ignores `LIGHT` and `STRUCTURE_VOID` during schematic scanning, preventing single floating dirt blocks under invisible light sources.
    - Center & Dynamic Radii: `centerX, centerZ` computed from projected non-air schematic blocks. `pitRadius = maxBlockDist + 1.5`, `outerRadius = pitRadius + clearance` (no extra bloated margins).
    - Strict Floor vs Overhang Separation:
      - Only blocks at floor level `worldY <= baseFloorY + 1` are fixed to `worldY - 1` with foundation underpinning downward.
      - Overhangs `worldY >= baseFloorY + 2` (tent roofs, scarecrow arms, arches) are NEVER fixed to `worldY - 1`; they are treated as pit floor `pitFloorY` so space between `pitFloorY + 1` and `worldY - 1` under overhangs is completely hollow air, eliminating stone and dirt pillars.
    - "Ellipse Up" Wall Profile: Flat horizontal tangent at pit floor ($t=0$) and steep elliptical climb ($curve = 1.0 - \sqrt{1 - t^2}$) over width `clearance` up to natural terrain ($t=1$).
    - Ping-Pong Diffusion (4 iterations): Smooths voxel stair-stepping only on transitional pit walls (`!isFixed`), preserving flat pit floor and outer terrain.
    - Terrain Modification & Safe Clearance: Places `topState` at `targetHeight` only when not occupied by a schematic block. Clearance loop clears strictly above `baseHeight` down to `baseHeight`, never digging ground under the structure.
  - Primitive 2D Arrays: Zero-allocation primitive grids `schematicLowestY[W][D]`, `heightMap[W][D]`, `originalHeights[W][D]`, `isFixed[W][D]`.
  - Fast BlockType Sets & Snowy Cache: Static `NATURAL_TERRAIN_TYPES`, `FOLIAGE_CACHE`, and `SNOWY_PROPERTY`.
  - Powder Snow & Gravel Fix: Replaced `topState` of `POWDER_SNOW` with `SNOW_BLOCK` and mapped `GRAVEL -> STONE` in `FILLER_MAP`.
  - Logging: Added SLF4J logger `Schematics#Smoothing`.
- OnlineTracker Player Iteration:
  - Rewrote `OnlineTracker.getNearbyPlayers(Location, double)` and `forEachNearby` to iterate `world.getPlayers()` instead of global proxy `SNAPSHOT` or `World.getNearbyEntities`.
  - Computes `dx * dx + dy * dy + dz * dz <= radiusSq` without allocating `Location` objects.
